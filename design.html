The Gulf — Lead story

Iran widens the war to Kuwait

Tehran fires on American drone and missile sites at Ali Al Salem air base, its first attack on Kuwait since September — two days after the United States bombed an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island. Bahrain says it has now turned back Iranian attacks three times in a week.

KUWAIT says its defences intercepted the drones; no damage reported.  BAHRAIN reports a third interception in seven days.  TEHRAN claims American casualties; Washington has confirmed none.

Shenzhen — Today

Three leaders, one table, first time ever

Trump, Xi and Putin sit down together today at the APEC summit — the first three-way meeting of the American, Chinese and Russian leaders. Nuclear arms, Ukraine and Iran are on the table; the Kremlin says one hour is set aside. The three shook hands at Wednesday’s welcome event.

Washington

Jeffries nominated, set to be first Black Speaker

House Democrats nominate Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker, 251–11 in closed-door voting on Wednesday. The New York congressman is on course to take the gavel on 3 January, the first Black lawmaker ever to hold it, when the new Congress convenes.

Business

Anthropic’s $81bn debut, the biggest IPO ever

The maker of the Claude AI assistant priced its shares at $83 on Wednesday, valuing it at $1.5 trillion, and starts trading today. The raise tops every flotation before it — ahead of SpaceX’s $75bn in June.

Gaza

Ground push in Gaza as four nations demand aid in

Israeli troops launch a ground operation in central Gaza, an area left under last year’s ceasefire. Australia, Canada, Italy and Britain jointly call for unrestricted aid access as flooding deepens the suffering.

Europe

EU offers Ukraine a €45bn loan, Russia to pay

Brussels proposes a €45 billion loan to Kyiv, to be repaid from Russian reparations once Moscow pays them. The plan lands as the war’s costs keep rising and the Shenzhen talks weigh a settlement.
